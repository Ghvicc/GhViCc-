PARTNERSHIP • COMMUNITY IMPACT

Creativity can become contribution.

GhViƆɔ partnered with the Rotaract Club of Kikuyu as a sponsor for their installation ceremony.

As part of the collaboration, GhViƆɔ produced an original art piece by Louis Mulema which was auctioned during the event.

The proceeds from the auction went toward the club's projects, connecting creative expression with tangible community impact.

For GhViƆɔ, this represents an important part of what collaboration can mean: creating something that doesn't simply look good, but contributes beyond itself.
